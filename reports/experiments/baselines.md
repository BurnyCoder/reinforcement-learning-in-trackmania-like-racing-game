**Question.** Floor (random actions, untrained network) and reference (scripted driver).

## Methodology

Training-free drivers scored by the same evaluator: uniformly random actions and an untrained network (the floor; several random seeds), and the frozen scripted driver (pure pursuit + curvature speed profile, two parameters tuned on validation candidates), whose times are the reference of the metric.

Metric: success-weighted speed (SWS) and finish rate; intervals are 95 % two-level bootstrap intervals (10,000 resamples of seeds and tracks). See `reports/preregistration.md` for the binding protocol.
