**Question.** How much time does a generalist lose against single-track specialists?

## Methodology

The standard recipe trained on one campaign track each (from scratch, and fine-tuned from the seed-1 generalist), compared on that track with the generalist zero-shot and the scripted driver, and also scored on the test tracks.

Metric: success-weighted speed (SWS) and finish rate; intervals are 95 % two-level bootstrap intervals (10,000 resamples of seeds and tracks). See `reports/preregistration.md` for the binding protocol.
