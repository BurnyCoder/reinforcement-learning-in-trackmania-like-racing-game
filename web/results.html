    Preregistered hypotheses

    Decision rule (preregistration): refuted if the 95 % interval lies below the threshold; confirmed if the Holm-adjusted one-sided t-test p-value is below 0.05; otherwise inconclusive; not yet tested until all preregistered seeds have test results.

    Glossary

    SWS (success-weighted speed)
    Mean over tracks of finished × (scripted driver's time ÷ agent's time). 1.0 = finishes every track as fast as the scripted driver; 0 = finishes nothing.
    Finish rate
    Share of tracks finished within 3× the reference time without falling off or stalling.
    95 % interval
    Two-level bootstrap: seeds and tracks resampled with replacement. With one seed it reflects track sampling only.
    Seed
    One independent training run. More seeds show how much results depend on luck.
